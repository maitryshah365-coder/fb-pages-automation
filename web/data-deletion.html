User Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Meta Platform policies, users have the right to request deletion of their data collected or processed by the Facebook Pages Automation Application.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request permanent deletion of all associated tokens, session data, or cached analytics, please follow either of these methods:

Method 1: Revoke Access via Facebook Settings (Instant Deletion)

  1. Go to your Facebook Profile's Settings & Privacy ➔ Settings.
  2. Navigate to Apps and Websites.
  3. Find Mia Auto Fleet (or the automation app).
  4. Click Remove to immediately revoke all data access and trigger automated data purge.

Method 2: Email Data Deletion Request

Send an email to miashah121298@gmail.com with the subject "Facebook Data Deletion Request" including your Page ID or User ID. We will permanently purge any associated logs and records within 24 hours.

For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.
